You can check for flat feet at home with the wet test: wet the sole of your foot, stand on a piece of cardboard or a dark towel, and look at the print. If you see almost the whole sole, with little or no curve on the inner side, you likely have flat feet. Other signs: your feet tire quickly, your arches or heels ache after standing, or your shoes wear down on the inside edge. A podiatrist can confirm it with a quick exam.

Sources: Cleveland Clinic, "Flat Feet (Pes Planus)" (https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/flat-feet)
