For flat feet, look for stability shoes with firm medial support.

1. **Brooks Adrenaline GTS 24**: my top pick for most runners with flat feet. GuideRails keep the knee aligned. Around $140.
2. **Hoka Arahi 7**: lightweight stability without a rigid post. $145.
3. **ASICS Gel-Kayano 31**: plush, maximum support, and often on sale at Running Warehouse for $129.
4. **Stridewell Arch Runner**: a newer option with a built-in arch cradle. Reviewers mention it runs narrow. $120 at stridewell.example.

Sources: Runner's World, "Best Running Shoes for Flat Feet" (https://www.runnersworld.com/gear/best-shoes-for-flat-feet); RTINGS, "The 6 Best Running Shoes for Flat Feet" (https://www.rtings.com/running-shoes/reviews/best/flat-feet)
