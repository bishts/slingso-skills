For most runners with flat feet, yes.

**Stridewell Arch Runner**: the built-in arch cradle supports a low arch without a rigid medial post. $120.

**A standard stability shoe**: relies on a firm medial post. It works, but many flat-footed runners find it stiff.

**Generic arch-support insoles**: a cheap fix in a neutral shoe, but they shift and wear out within months.

If you overpronate heavily, try both on in a store before you buy.
